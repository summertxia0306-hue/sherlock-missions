## 1. 本课核心目标

### 核心交际任务

能介绍自己、介绍朋友或家人，并说出年龄和一项能力。

### 核心句型

- This is ...
- His/Her name is ...
- My name is ...
- He's/She's ...
- He/She can ...
- Nice to meet you.
- Nice to meet you too.

### 核心语法

- 物主代词：my / his / her
- 所有格：Peter's
- be 动词：is / am；He's / She's
- 情态动词：can + 动词原形
- 年龄表达：He's/She's + age；ten years old

## 2. 课本页逐页抽取

### p.2 Look and say

页面主题：见面、介绍兄弟姐妹、告别。

核心表达：

- Good morning, Peter.
- Good morning, Kitty.
- This is my sister.
- Her name is Sally.
- She's twelve.
- This is my brother.
- His name is Paul.
- He's only six.
- My name is Kitty.
- I'm Peter's classmate.
- We are going to the park.
- Goodbye, Kitty.
- See you.

课件可拆句型：

- This is my sister/brother/friend/classmate.
- Her name is Sally.
- His name is Paul.
- She's twelve.
- He's six.
- My name is Kitty.

### p.3 Look and learn / Ask and answer

页面主题：数字 11-16；介绍朋友的年龄和能力。

数字词：

- eleven = 11
- twelve = 12
- thirteen = 13
- fourteen = 14
- fifteen = 15
- sixteen = 16

核心表达：

- This is my friend.
- His name's Danny.
- He's nine.
- He can ride a bicycle.
- Her name's Tracy.
- She's thirteen.
- She can skip.

课件可拆句型：

- This is my ...
- His/Her name's ...
- He's/She's ... (age)
- He/She can ...

课文复现短语：

- ride a bicycle
- skip
- talk about him/her
- show a photo of your friend

### p.4 Say and act · A new classmate

页面主题：新同学入班、自我介绍、学生号码。

核心表达：

- Good morning, class.
- Good morning, Miss Fang.
- This is your new classmate.
- Her name's Jill.
- She's ten years old.
- Her student number is sixteen.
- Hi, I'm Jill.
- You can sit here, Jill.
- OK. Thank you, Miss Fang.
- Nice to meet you, Jill.
- My name's Kitty.
- I'm nine.
- My student number is fifteen.
- Hi, Kitty. Nice to meet you too.

课文复现短语：

- new classmate
- ten years old
- student number
- sit here

### p.5 Look and read · Jill

页面主题：阅读 Jill 的个人介绍。

核心表达：

- We have a new classmate, Jill.
- She's ten years old.
- She lives near our school.
- She walks to school every day.
- She likes reading.
- She can play basketball well.
- She can dance too.
- She likes dancing.

阅读判断答案：

| 题号 | 句子 | 答案 |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Jill is a new student. | True |
| 2 | Jill's home is near the school. | True |
| 3 | Jill likes reading and dancing. | True |
| 4 | Jill can play football. | False |

课文复现短语：

- live near our school
- walk to school every day
- like reading
- play basketball well
- like dancing

语法提醒：

- lives / walks / likes 是第三人称单数，本课可轻触，不宜一上来讲太重。
- can 后面用动词原形：can play / can dance。

### p.6 Listen and enjoy / Learn the sound

页面主题：姓名拼读；-sk 发音。

Listen and enjoy 核心表达：

- My name is Joe.
- J-O-E, Joe.
- What about you?
- My name's Rose.
- R-O-S-E, Rose.
- Nice to meet you, Joe.

Learn the sound：

- -sk: desk, mask

语音小诗核心句：

- Martin sits at his desk.
- Making a funny mask.
- His grandma asks.
- She puts on the mask.

## 4. 给互动课件的结构建议

### Lesson 1 可拆成 5 个页面

1. 课文导入：Kitty meets Sally and Paul.
2. 单词能量站：eleven-sixteen + classmate + student number。
3. 句型训练：This is my friend. His/Her name is ...
4. 跟读导读：Jill 的短文 + -sk 发音。
5. 小复盘：介绍一个朋友或家人。

### 第一版 HTML 不宜过重

建议第一版只做：

- 6 个数字词；
- 4 个身份/介绍词：classmate, student, number, friend；
- 3 个能力短语：ride a bicycle, skip, play basketball；
- 3 个句型：This is ... / His-Her name is ... / He-She can ...

剩余内容放到纸面和第二天复现，避免孩子一次吃太满。

这个判断最可能错在哪：如果学校老师第一课实际要求背完整 p.2-p.6 全部课文，那么这里只做“轻量先修”会偏浅；但按暑期预习和孩子负荷控制，先抓数字、介绍句型和 Jill 阅读核心，比一次塞满整课更稳。
