## W01D41 · 听力复习 · Meeting new people

M1U1：人物介绍、姓名、年龄、学号和my/your/his/her

### 听音选词

1. 原文：classmate；答案：classmate
2. 原文：eleven；答案：eleven
3. 原文：student；答案：student
4. 原文：number；答案：number

### 听句判断

5. 原文：This is my new classmate.；屏显：This is my new classmate.；答案：相同
6. 原文：Her name is Jill.；屏显：His name is Jill.；答案：不同
7. 原文：My student number is fourteen.；屏显：My student number is fourteen.；答案：相同
8. 原文：Jill lives near our school.；屏显：Jill lives far from our school.；答案：不同

### 听问句选答语

9. 问句：What's your name?；答案：My name is Alice.
10. 问句：How old are you?；答案：I'm ten.
11. 问句：What's your student number?；答案：It's thirteen.
12. 问句：Who is she?；答案：She's my classmate.

### 听对话选答案

13. 对话：This is my friend, Jill. / Hello, Jill.；问题：Who is Jill?；答案：A friend.
14. 对话：What's his name? / His name is Joe.；问题：What is the boy's name?；答案：Joe.
15. 对话：How old is Kitty? / She is eleven.；问题：How old is Kitty?；答案：Eleven.
16. 对话：Does Jill walk to school? / Yes, she does.；问题：How does Jill go to school?；答案：She walks.

### 听短文判断

**短文原文**：This is Alice. She is ten. Her student number is fifteen. Peter is her classmate. He is eleven. His student number is twelve.

17. 判断句：Alice is ten.；答案：正确
18. 判断句：Alice's student number is twelve.；答案：错误
19. 判断句：Peter is Alice's classmate.；答案：正确
20. 判断句：Peter is eleven.；答案：正确

2. 原文：write；答案：write
3. 原文：swim；答案：swim
4. 原文：jump；答案：jump

### 听句判断

5. 原文：I can paint a picture.；屏显：I can paint a picture.；答案：相同
6. 原文：She can swim well.；屏显：She can swims well.；答案：不同
7. 原文：He can run fast.；屏显：He can run fast.；答案：相同
8. 原文：The bird can fly high.；屏显：The bird can jump high.；答案：不同

9. 问句：What can you do?；答案：I can draw.
10. 问句：Can she swim?；答案：Yes, she can.
11. 问句：Can the fish fly?；答案：No, it can't.
12. 问句：What can he do?；答案：He can run.

### 听对话选答案

13. 对话：Can you draw, Ben? / Yes. I can draw a robot.；问题：What can Ben draw?；答案：A robot.

15. 对话：What can Peter do? / He can read and write.；问题：Can Peter write?；答案：Yes, he can.
16. 对话：Can this bird run? / No. It can fly high.；问题：What can the bird do?；答案：Fly high.

### 听短文判断

**短文原文**：Alice can read and write. She likes drawing. Ben can run fast, but he cannot swim. Their little bird can fly high.

17. 判断句：Alice can read.；答案：正确
18. 判断句：Alice likes swimming.；答案：错误
19. 判断句：Ben can swim.；答案：错误
20. 判断句：The bird can fly high.；答案：正确

### 听对话选答案

13. 对话：I'm thirsty. / Have some water. / Thank you.；问题：What does the girl need?；答案：Water.

20. 判断句：Ben is sad at the end.；答案：错误
